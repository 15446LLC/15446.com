Module 1 — Reconciliation Health
Last Reconciled Date

See when each account was last reconciled, with color-coded health indicators based on how recent it was.

Reconciliation Integrity

If a previously-reconciled transaction is later edited or un-reconciled, it surfaces as an unreconciled gap before the last reconciliation date — catching broken reconciliations as part of the same check.

Unreconciled Transaction Gaps

Identify transactions dated before the last reconciliation date that were never reconciled — a sign of retroactive edits.

Sum & Earliest Date

See the total dollar amount and earliest date of unreconciled transactions, so you know the scope of the issue at a glance.

Module 2 — Undeposited Funds Hygiene
Balance Check

Checks the Undeposited Funds account balance first — if it's zero, there's nothing sitting unswept and no further scan is needed.

Unswept Payments

When the balance is nonzero, finds the specific payments that haven't been matched to a bank deposit yet, misstating cash on the balance sheet until they're swept in.

Aged by Severity

Buckets unswept payments by age — under 30 days, 30 days to 12 months, and over 12 months — so a brand-new payment doesn't read the same as a year-old one.

Module 3 — Unapplied Payments & Credits
Customer Side

Payments and credit memos not yet applied to an invoice — money received but still sitting as an unapplied credit, overstating open AR.

Vendor Side

Vendor credits not applied to a bill, and vendor payments with no linked bill — understating what's actually owed or leaving cash unaccounted for.

Aged by Severity

Same age-bucketed approach as Module 2 — a few recent days is normal processing lag, anything over 12 months is a real hygiene problem.
